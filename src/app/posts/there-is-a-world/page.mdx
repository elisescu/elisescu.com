# There&apos;s a world

_April 10, 2025_

There is a world out there, or in there, or somewhere, where [Of Monsters And Men - Crystals](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XUClIslXKZo) and [Efterklang - Cutting Ice to Snow](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sKccgM0VN0Q) are the Universe anthems.

![Train window](/assets/train-window.jpeg)

One where this is image is not one of the sad Eastern European country side seen throught the eyes of a tired dad coffeinated with cheap cofee, but one of a wonderful view where the warm sun is shining brighly over the grass frozen by a late winter - all seen through the eyes of a ethernally grateful mind.

And sometimes the two worlds meet like two sisters reunited after a long time.
